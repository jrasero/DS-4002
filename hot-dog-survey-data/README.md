# Project 0: Is a hot dog a sandwich?
This project will explore whether 1/4 of full-time UVA undergraduate students believe a hot dog is a sandwich.

## Data Dictionary
c: date and time of response (time series)\
\
Year at UVA: one of seven choices (1st Year, 2nd Year, 3rd Year, 4th Year, Graduate Student, Faculty/Staff, Other/Not Affiliated)\
\
What is your Gender Identity?: one of four choices (Man, Woman, Nonbinary, Prefer not to say), or an open field (Other:)\
\
What Schools are you a Part of?: any combination of the 12 schools at UVA\
\
Is a Hotdog a sandwich?: Yes or No\
\
How strongly do you feel about your previous answer?: Likert scale 1-5\
\
How frequently do you eat hot dogs?: one of four choices (Never, Rarely (ex. once a year), Occassionally (ex. once every few months), Frequently (ex. multiple times a month))
